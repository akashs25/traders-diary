⏰ Daily Trading Workflow @ 9:15 AM

  1. Get Opening Price: At 9:15 AM, note the LTP (Last Traded Price) of your stock/index from your trading terminal
  2. Enter Anchor Price: Enter this LTP in the "Anchor Price" field above (e.g., ₹932.70)
  3. Choose Direction:
    • Bullish View? → Click "📈 Upward (Bullish)" to see Support levels below the anchor for Long positions
    • Bearish View? → Click "📉 Downward (Bearish)" to see Resistance levels above the anchor for Short positions
  4. Set Your Levels:
    • For LONG: Set buy orders near S1, S2, S3 (support levels). Use R1, R2 as profit targets
    • For SHORT: Set sell orders near R1, R2, R3 (resistance levels). Use S1, S2 as profit targets
  5. Key Points:
    • Cardinal Cross (Teal): 90°, 180°, 270°, 360° - Strongest S/R levels
    • Ordinal Cross (Yellow): 45°, 135°, 225°, 315° - Intermediate levels
    • Perimeter (Light Blue): Extreme exhaustion zone - reversal expected
  6. Export & Save: Click "📊 Export to CSV" or "📑 Export to Excel" to save your daily levels for reference

⚠️ Pro Tip: Update the anchor price throughout the day if price moves significantly (±2-3%) to recalibrate levels

📖 How It Works

Upward Series (Long): Price = (√Anchor + (Step × 0.125))² — Each step adds 45° vibration

Downward Series (Short): Price = (√Anchor - (Step × 0.125))² — Each step subtracts 45° vibration

Spiral Logic: Starts from center (Row 4, Col 4) and moves clockwise: Right → Down → Left → Up
